SLIDE 13

Final EDA Takeaways

  • Weak raw linear relationships for passenger count, traffic, car condition, and airport distance.
  • Distance is sensitive to extreme observations; robust analysis reveals a much stronger association.
  • 7 PM is the demand peak; 5 AM is the highest observed average-fare hour with fewer observations.
  • Weather mean-distance differences are heavily influenced by extreme values.
  • Data-quality limitations should be considered before modeling.

These are EDA observations, not causal claims.
